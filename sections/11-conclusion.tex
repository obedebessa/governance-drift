\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Reconciliation solved configuration drift so well that its green status has
come to stand for a property it does not check. This paper named the
difference: governance drift, the divergence of operational reality from the
state, policy context, authorization basis, intent, and assumptions under
which it was approved. We separated five substantive drift components and a
transversal epistemic evidence condition formally; established,
by constructed counterexample and by a definitional-dependency argument,
that configuration equality does not imply governance consistency and that
no configuration-pair detector can decide it; modeled governance divergence as
an irreducibly component-wise substantive vector plus observability mask,
anchored to a recorded approval event;
organized detection as cumulative evidence tiers; and checked, in an
executed and fully reproducible closed-world study with class-set ground
truth and benign governance churn in its control, that a
definition-faithful reference implementation realizes the model's
detectability staircase: configuration comparison decides exactly one of
twelve drift scenarios and each governance tier detects precisely the components
whose deciding facts it carries, earning zero false alarms against
governance noise. RQ0 is thereby answered with its
conditions stated: governance drift is formally representable (the vector
$\GD$), measurable exactly where the admitted basis was recorded (coverage,
GCC, bounds everything else), and detectable to the depth of the evidence
tiers an estate maintains. The repeated Kubernetes/GitOps laboratory executed
20 repetitions of all nine scenarios at 0.5-, 2-, and 10-s cadences on a
pinned Kind/Flux/Kyverno stack. It detected 538 of 540 observations; all 538
detected outputs exactly matched the expected sets, yielding zero conditional
Hamming loss. Twenty benign windows
covering six change families produced zero substantive alarms and zero
epistemic warnings in 543 polls. Separate
injection, onset, detection, and evidence clocks show that actuation and DDL
are distinct: for example, S6's median actuation was 10.05~s while median DDL
was 0.54~s at the default cadence. These results establish bounded
realizability and within-laboratory behavior, not natural occurrence,
prevalence, production reliability, or universal latency distributions.
An independent no-join composite diagnoses only 9/12 closed-world scenario
sets versus 12/12 for the history-aware join, isolating the increment from
alarm volume. The live compound extension reaches exact final vectors in
45/45 cadence observations and exposes why first alert and vector completion
are distinct: S10's median DDL is 1.33~s but its median complete diagnosis is
7.88~s. A 30-minute benign soak adds 4,680 polls without substantive or
epistemic alarms; it strengthens the bounded specificity check without being
misrepresented as a production-rate estimate.

The definitions are offered for reuse; the matrix is offered as a template
for evaluating any governance-drift detector; and the central lesson is
offered to practice in one sentence: a system can match its manifest
perfectly and still be running outside everything that made it legitimate---%
and whether an organization can \emph{see} that is an architectural choice,
made tier by tier, that this paper makes explicit.
